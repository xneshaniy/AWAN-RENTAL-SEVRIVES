PART 1 — FOR YOUR CUSTOMERS (How to book a car)
================================================================

WAY 1 — BOOK BY WHATSAPP (Easiest)
  1. Open the website on your phone or computer
  2. Tap the green "Book on WhatsApp" button
  3. Type your message: where, when, which car
  4. We reply fast and confirm everything

WAY 2 — BOOK ON THE WEBSITE FORM
  1. Tap "Book a Car" or "Find Your Ride"
  2. Choose: pickup place, drop place, date and time
  3. Pick your car (see prices and photos)
  4. Fill in your name and phone number
  5. Tap Submit — we call you back to confirm

WAY 3 — CALL US
  Call: +92 305 6846811
  Or WhatsApp: +92 305 6846811

WHAT CUSTOMERS GET
  - A clean car, ready to go
  - Self-drive (you drive) or with our driver
  - Airport pick-up and drop-off
  - Daily, weekly or monthly rental — your choice

QUESTIONS / ANSWERS (Common ones)
  Q: What do I need to rent?
  A: Your driving licence (for self-drive) and CNIC or passport.

  Q: Can I get the car delivered?
  A: Yes — in Islamabad, Rawalpindi and Lahore. Ask when booking.

  Q: How do I pay?
  A: No online payment needed. We arrange payment when we confirm
     your booking — simple and easy.

PART 2 — FOR YOU (The owner, how to run the site)
================================================================

ADMIN LOGIN (your private panel)
  URL:      http://localhost:3000/admin/login  (local PC)
  Live:     https://awanrentalservice.org/admin/login
  Email:    info@awanrentalservice.org
  Password: admin123   (change it in Settings later)

WHAT YOU CAN DO IN THE ADMIN PANEL
  Dashboard        See everything at a glance
  Vehicles         Add your real cars (name, price, photos)
  Bookings         See all booking requests, call customers
  Self Drive Gallery     Upload your car photos and videos
  Homepage Showcase      Upload photos/videos for the homepage
  Site Settings    Phone, WhatsApp, hours, Facebook, etc.

ADDING YOUR REAL CARS
  1. Admin > Vehicles > "Add New Vehicle"
  2. Enter: name, model, year, seats, daily price
  3. Upload 2-5 photos (tap "Upload Photo")
  4. Tap "Save Vehicle" — it appears on the website instantly

ADDING PHOTOS AND VIDEOS
  1. Admin > Self Drive Gallery  (for /self-drive page)
     OR Admin > Homepage Showcase  (for homepage)
  2. Tap "Upload from device" and pick your photos or videos
  3. Or paste a link (https://...) for each item
  4. They show on the website automatically

INSTALL THE APP ON YOUR PHONE
  - Open the website on your phone
  - Android: tap "Install" banner (or menu > Add to Home Screen)
  - iPhone: tap Share > Add to Home Screen > Add
  - The "Awan Rental" app icon appears on your phone

LOCAL COMPUTER (for you / developer)
  1. Open the project folder
  2. Run: npm run dev
  3. Open: http://localhost:3000
  4. Admin: http://localhost:3000/admin/login
